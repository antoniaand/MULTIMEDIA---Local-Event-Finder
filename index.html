About EventsHUB

A simple idea that grew into a place where you can discover what’s happening near you — from music nights and food fairs to workshops and city tours.

Explore the map Browse events

✨ Our story

It started with one problem: events were scattered everywhere — Instagram stories, random posters, and different websites. We wanted one clean place to explore them, see locations quickly, and get a feel for the vibe before going.

That’s why we added audio previews: a short promo can tell you more than a paragraph.

How it started

We began by listing a few events we’d personally want to attend. Then we added a map, so you can see where everything is at a glance. Finally, we added audio previews to make the experience feel more “real” — like a mini trailer for each event.

What we believe

  • Simple browsing: no clutter, just useful info.
  • Fast decisions: map + quick previews helps you pick.
  • Local energy: highlight events that bring people together.

What’s next

We’re planning to add short video previews and better filters (music, food, tech, art), so it’s even easier to find your type of event.
